# Nearby comparisons localize the mean

Extract the ZIP and open index.html. The explorer works offline. Rotate the three-dimensional feasible region with the slider or by dragging. Remove any one close comparison and see the region extend toward the corresponding face of the parameter cube.

With all six signed coordinate cuts at distance 1/16 and the true mean at the origin, the greatest feasible distance is √3/16. Removing one cut admits a displacement of length 3/4, beyond the test radius 1/2. The illustrated comparison pairs are deterministic.
