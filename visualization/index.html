Why six directions suffice here

The cuts are at signed coordinate values ±1/16. Every unit direction has a coordinate magnitude at least 1/√3, so one of the six close cuts bounds motion along that direction. The most distant feasible points are the corners of the small cube. Without one cut the box extends to the corresponding face of the parameter cube. The theorem obtains close cuts with random directions and the estimator uses every observed comparison.
