# Internal mathematical review

The review checks the independence and Gaussian variance of midpoints, the sign of the halfspace conversion, the pair KL, the reference conditional cross entropy, and the binary-entropy reduction to Euclidean risk. It checks small offsets, constant cap probability, VC trace counts with a second sample, and tail integration including the cube boundary and one-dimensional sphere. The midpoint estimator gives the small-sample regime. The offset selection in the proof uses the truth only for analysis.

The exact replay corroborates the algebra, the crossover and a deterministic cap-cover example. The manuscript gives the full proof. External peer review and formal verification have not been conducted.
