# Claim scope

Both points of each of the n independent pairs are observed and sampled independently from the same Gaussian distribution with unknown mean in [−1,1]ᵈ and known identity covariance. The noiseless label indicates the closer point. The risk is the expectation of Euclidean norm, with a supremum over the mean and an infimum over estimators. The two rates hold up to absolute multiplicative constants.

The statistical experiment fixes the sample law and the label rule. Active queries, shared comparison items, preference noise, thresholded single-sample feedback, other covariances and arbitrary bounded parameter sets are outside the claim. The theorem gives rates up to constants. It leaves numerical sharp constants and computational complexity open.
